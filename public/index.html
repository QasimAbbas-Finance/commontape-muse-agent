Ask Meta's AI for a cleaner, get a real booking.

Send a voice note, a photo of your home or a few words. A Muse agent works out the job, finds cleaners on CommonTape that can do it, shows the exact price, checks the booking before it makes it, and books a slot.

This demo books CommonTape Demo Cleaning, a pretend business that serves ZIP 10001 in New York. No real cleaner is ever contacted, and demo bookings are deleted after a day. Everyone trying the demo shares it, so it can be busy.

Finding a cleaner never books anything. Booking is a second step, and only when you say so.

Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, 26 Oct – 4 Nov 2026, with Meta's Muse Spark and Muse Voice Transcribe on CommonTape. The code is public.
